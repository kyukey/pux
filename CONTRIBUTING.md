# Contributing to pux

Issues are very welcome. Pull requests are not accepted, so please open an issue instead.

pux is a one-person project, so replies and fixes may take a while. I'll try to read every issue, but not every one will lead to a change.

## Found a bug?

[Open an issue](https://github.com/kyukey/pux/issues/new) with:

- the output of `pux version`
- your system, like macOS 15, Ubuntu 24.04 or Windows 11
- what you ran, what you expected, and what happened instead

## Have an idea?

Open an issue that describes the problem you want solved, not just the feature. It helps to know how you would use it.

pux aims to stay small and focused, so ideas that do not fit may be declined. If you want something pux will not add, you are free to fork it and make it your own under the terms of the [license](LICENSE).

## Found a security problem?

Please do not open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to report it privately.
